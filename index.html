About Dine with Rika

Dine with Rika is a catering company dedicated to providing clients with exquisite, delicious and memorable food experiences. Founded in 2017 from a passion for food and a desire to create catering experiences that bring people together.

We work closely with our clients to create menus that complement the occasion, theme, guest profile and desired atmosphere. Our focus is on quality food, beautiful presentation, professional preparation and exceptional service.
